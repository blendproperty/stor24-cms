# Satoshi font credits

The CMS interface uses the unmodified `Satoshi-Variable.ttf` from the approved
STOR24 public website and CRM brand pack, weights 300–900. See
`docs/STOR24_BRAND_CI.md` for provenance and byte-identity verification.
The older Handover files below are historical assets and are not the CMS font.

Satoshi is distributed by Indian Type Foundry (ITF) through Fontshare. ITF owns the applicable font trademarks and copyrights.

The handover, MRI accounting and identity review screens use the unmodified official regular (400), medium (500), and bold (700) WOFF2 files downloaded on 18 September 2026.

Source: https://api.fontshare.com/v2/css?f[]=satoshi@400,500,700&display=swap
Font family: https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/satoshi
Terms identified in the font metadata: https://fontshare.com/terms

These are separate static weights. The pre-existing Satoshi-Variable.ttf remains unchanged.
